First Android release.
* For every Pocket AMP, whatever the brand: M-VAVE, Donner, Coolmusic PIXIE1, New Bee SK17.
* Connects over Bluetooth (pair from the app) or USB-C.
* Edit presets live, name and rate them, back up and restore.
* Send your own IRs and A2 .nam amp captures to the pedal.
