TONE3000 captures and IRs, inside the app

TONE3000 is where guitarists share free NAM captures of amps and pedals, and IRs of cabinets. Pocket AMP Tools shows it right in the Library: find a sound, get it, and send it to one of the pedal's 20 AMP or 20 CAB slots, without downloading and converting files by hand.

Download the app tone3000.com

Version 0.6.0 or newer. Free TONE3000 account and an internet connection needed. Pocket AMP Tools is a community tool, not affiliated with TONE3000 or any brand of the pedal.

Get a capture, step by step

  1. Open the Library section. Above your amp files, click Get captures from TONE3000; above your IRs (the Cabinets tab), Get IRs from TONE3000.
  2. The first time, click Sign in with TONE3000. The app opens tone3000.com in your browser with a short code; approve it there with your account. The app never sees your password.
  3. Browse Trending, Latest, Favorites, Downloaded or Mine, or type an amp, a pedal or a creator in the search box: it looks through all of TONE3000.
  4. Click Get on a tone. If it holds several captures (clean, crunch, lead…), pick one.
  5. The file lands in your library, checked like any other. Click Send to pedal… on it and pick a slot.
  6. In the Presets section, choose the new model in the AMP or CAB block. You hear it live.

Only what the pedal can play

The pedal runs one small, fixed network for its amps, and an A2 capture already contains it. So when you get captures, the lists and the search show only A2 NAM tones; when you get IRs, only IR tones. A line under the list says how many tones were left out, so nothing disappears without a word.

IRs on TONE3000 come in many shapes. The app converts each one to the format the pedal takes (44.1 kHz, mono, 24-bit, 2048 samples) as it adds it, and keeps a file already in that format byte for byte. More on both in the IR and NAM guide.

Captures with their cabinet

Many tones on TONE3000 are an amp captured together with its cabinet, marked amp-cab (or a whole rig). Played through the pedal's own CAB block, they would go through two cabinets and sound boxy. The app remembers which captures those are, and turns the CAB block off when you pick one in the editor, and in the presets that already play its slot when you send it. As with any edit, nothing is stored until you write the presets. A .nam you add yourself is recognized too when its file says it holds a cabinet.

Creators and licenses

Every tone shows who made it, what was captured and its license, and the arrow next to Get opens its page on tone3000.com, where you can like it, read its notes and see how to credit it. Captures are the work of their creators: respect the license if you share a recording or a preset made with one.

Your account and privacy

  • You sign in on tone3000.com itself (OAuth device sign-in). The app gets a key to your account, never your password.
  • The key stays on your device, readable only by you on Linux and macOS, until you click Sign out. It is not synced to your other devices.
  • Nothing is downloaded until you click Get, and files are not kept anywhere but your library.

Questions

Do I need a TONE3000 account?

Yes, a free one: the lists, the search and the downloads are those of your account. You can still add .nam and WAV files you got any other way with Add .nam files… and Add WAV files….

Why are some tones missing from the lists?

They are not A2 captures (or, for IRs, not IRs), so the pedal cannot play them. A1 and other NAM files would have to be retrained to the pedal's network, which the app does not do.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, on Android the window fills the screen, and the sign-in link opens in the phone's browser with one tap. With sync on, what you get on the phone shows up in the library of your computer too.

Can I send to the pedal over Bluetooth?

The app can, on Android and Linux, but sending a capture or an IR over the radio was not yet tried on a pedal: use the USB cable for the library if you can, and tell us if Bluetooth worked for you.

I build the app myself. Do I need my own TONE3000 key?

A fork that wants its own builds with --dart-define=TONE3000_CLIENT_ID=...; the official key is public and works out of the box.
