Update the Pocket AMP firmware on Mac, Linux, Windows or Android

M-VAVE's own updater runs only on Windows. Pocket AMP Tools is a free, open source app that installs the official firmware from any of these systems over a plain USB cable, without drivers, and keeps your presets.

Download the app

Community tool, not affiliated with M-VAVE or any other brand of the pedal. An update that is interrupted can leave the pedal unusable: use it at your own risk.

Before you start

  • A USB data cable. Many USB cables only charge. If the app finds no pedal, the cable is the first thing to change. On a phone you need a USB OTG cable.
  • A charged pedal. The update takes about two minutes and the pedal must stay on.
  • Nothing else using MIDI. Close the M-VAVE app, your DAW and browser tabs that use Web MIDI.
  • No driver. The pedal is a standard USB-MIDI device.

Install the app

SystemHow to run
macOS 11+Unzip, then right-click Pocket AMP Tools and choose Open (the app is not signed). If macOS says it is damaged, run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "Pocket AMP Tools.app" once.
Linux x64chmod +x the AppImage and run it. Needs GTK 3 and ALSA. Your user needs access to /dev/snd/midi* (usually the audio group or a desktop session).
Windows 10/11Unzip and run pocket-amp-tools.exe. If SmartScreen warns, choose More info → Run anyway.
Android 8+Install the APK. Updating from a phone was not reported yet; a computer is the tested path.

Update, step by step

  1. Connect the pedal with the data cable and turn it on.
  2. Open Pocket AMP Tools. It detects the pedal and shows the installed version.
  3. Open the Firmware section and click Download from M-VAVE. The app fetches the current official file from M-VAVE's server. If you already have a .fwsc file, use Open .fwsc file… instead.
  4. Click Update firmware and confirm. The app first backs up all 80 presets and their EQ.
  5. Wait about two minutes. The pedal restarts twice: once into update mode, once into the new firmware.
  6. Click Restore my presets from before the update. Only the presets and EQ that changed are written back, and the result is read back to check it.

To check the installed version on the pedal itself, hold Home and press >.

Will I lose my presets?

A firmware update can overwrite some presets and their EQ. That is why the app takes a backup before it starts and offers to restore it afterwards. The backup is a plain JSON file in Documents/Pocket AMP Tools, listed in the Backups section, and you can save one yourself at any time.

Donner Pocket AMP, Coolmusic PIXIE1, New Bee SK17

These are the same pedal under another name, and owners of each one have updated theirs to V1.05 with this app. Two things to know before you do:

  • Afterwards the pedal reports itself as a Pocket AMP, and there may be no firmware of the original brand to go back to. The app asks for a separate confirmation for this.
  • Older firmware of another brand (PIXIE1 V0.07) has no EQ and stops at preset P79. The app keeps a raw copy of the presets and, after the update, can restore P01 to P79 from it. After updating from V0.07, M-VAVE says to switch to Speaker mode and load the Speaker mode preset in its app to get sound.

A pedal sold under a name the app does not know is never updated, because a different pedal that speaks the same protocol would be bricked by this firmware. Report yours on GitHub to get it listed.

If something goes wrong

The app finds no pedal

Try another cable (many are charge-only) and another USB port, and make sure no other app has the MIDI port open. On Windows only one app can open a MIDI device at a time.

It says the pedal already runs this version

The Pocket AMP does not reinstall the firmware it already has. The app checks the version first and tells you instead of starting.

The update stopped halfway

Do not unplug the pedal in a hurry. Reconnect it, open the app again and click Detect pedal. A pedal waiting in update mode is shown as update mode, and Resume update writes the firmware again from there. On Windows, two owners saw the first attempt stop in update mode and the second one finish.

Where does the firmware come from?

From M-VAVE. The app downloads the official file and checks the package, the pedal's identity and every packet before anything is written. It does not ship or modify the firmware.

After the update

The same app edits your presets live and loads your own IRs and NAM amp captures into the pedal.
