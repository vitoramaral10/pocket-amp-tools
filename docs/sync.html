Guide · Sync devices

Keep your phone and computer in sync

Get a capture on the phone at rehearsal and find it on the computer at home. Pocket AMP Tools keeps your devices the same by itself, talking to each other directly on your network: no account, no server, no cloud.

Download the app

Version 0.7.0 or newer on every device. Windows, macOS, Linux and Android.

Pair two devices

  1. Open the app on both, on the same Wi-Fi or network.
  2. On each, open Sync devices: the devices button at the top of the window, or the menu (⋮) on a phone. Turn on Keep my devices in sync.
  3. After a few seconds each device lists the other under Other devices on this network. Click Pair on one of them.
  4. Both screens show the same six-digit code. Check that it matches and click Codes match on both.

That is needed once per pair of devices. To add a third device, pair it with each of the others the same way.

What syncs, and what does not

SyncsStays on each device
The library: amp captures, IRs, and which file went to which slotThe assistant's key and settings
Preset backupsThe TONE3000 sign-in
Preset names, descriptions, labels and ratingsError-report consent and the app's settings

The presets themselves live in the pedal, so each device reads them from it. After pairing, the devices sync a few seconds after any change, with no button to press. The last edit of each item wins, and deleting a file on one device deletes it on the others.

When it syncs

Whenever the app is open on both devices, on the same network. With the app closed nothing syncs, and the next time both are open they catch up. On Android a notification stays up while sync is on, so the phone keeps syncing with the screen off; it goes away when you close the app from the recent apps.

Private by design

  • The devices talk directly to each other; nothing goes through the internet or anyone's server.
  • Everything is encrypted end to end (X25519 and ChaCha20-Poly1305). The six-digit check makes sure no other device on the network sits in the middle while pairing.
  • A device that was never paired gets nothing, and what arrives is checked like a file you add yourself.

If the devices do not see each other

  • Linux: a firewall such as ufw or firewalld blocks other devices. The Sync page says so and Let sync through opens the port to home and office networks only (private addresses), after the system asks for your password. Any other firewall needs port 47913 (TCP and UDP) allowed by hand.
  • Windows and macOS ask once whether the app may use the network: allow it.
  • Guest and office Wi-Fi often keep devices apart. Try your home network or the phone's hotspot.
  • Both devices need the app open with Keep my devices in sync on, and the same version of the app.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Pairing is between your devices, and nothing is stored anywhere else.

What happens if I edit the same preset note on two devices?

The later edit wins when they sync. The clocks of the devices decide what is later, so keep them on automatic time.

How do I stop syncing with a device?

Click the unlink button next to it under Paired devices and confirm. Nothing is deleted on either device; forget it on the other one too, or pair them again later.
