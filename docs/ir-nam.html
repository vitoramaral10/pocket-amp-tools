Guide · IRs and amp captures

Load your own IRs and NAM captures into the Pocket AMP

The pedal has 20 CAB slots for impulse responses and 20 AMP slots for neural amp captures. Pocket AMP Tools, a free and open source app, fills them with your own files from macOS, Linux, Windows or Android.

Download the app

Works the same on the M-VAVE Pocket AMP, Donner Pocket AMP, Coolmusic PIXIE1 and New Bee SK17. Community tool, not affiliated with any brand of the pedal or with TONE3000.

What the pedal accepts

FileGoes to
Impulse responseWAV in any shape; the app converts it to 44.1 kHz, mono, 24-bit, 2048 samplesOne of the 20 CAB slots
Amp captureAn A2 .nam file from TONE3000One of the 20 AMP slots

An A2 .nam file holds one network per size. Its smallest, the Lite, is the very network the pedal runs, so the app copies its weights into the pedal's own format without training or converting anything. A1 .nam files and any other shape do not fit the pedal and are refused, with the reason.

The pedal takes an IR in one format only: 44.1 kHz, mono, 24-bit, 2048 samples. A WAV in any other shape (another sample rate, stereo, 16 or 32-bit, float, longer or shorter) is converted when you add it: mixed to mono, resampled, cut to 2048 samples with a short fade or padded with silence, and kept at its level unless it would clip. A file already in the pedal's format is kept byte for byte. From its code, the firmware seems to keep only the first 1024 of the 2048 samples of an IR.

Straight from TONE3000

You do not have to download files by hand: Get captures from TONE3000 and Get IRs from TONE3000, in the Library, search and browse TONE3000 inside the app and put what you pick in the library, already checked. Captures made together with their cabinet (amp-cab) turn the CAB block off where they play. See the TONE3000 guide.

Send a file to the pedal

  1. Connect the pedal with a USB data cable and open Pocket AMP Tools.
  2. Open the Library section and add your files with the button at the top (Add .nam files… under Amps, Add WAV files… under Cabinets), or get them from TONE3000 with the button below it. They are copied into Documents/Pocket AMP Tools/Library, so the originals can go. With sync on, your other devices get them too.
  3. Click Send to pedal… on a file and pick a slot, or Load from library… on a slot and pick a file.
  4. In the Presets section, choose the new model in the AMP or CAB block. Models you uploaded show with their own names, and you hear the change live.

What happens to the slot

Sending writes to the pedal's flash and survives turning the pedal off. What was in the slot is gone for good: firmware V1.05 has no way to read an IR or a capture back out of the pedal. So the library records which file went to which slot, and the slot shows it. Slots filled by the factory or by another app have no file behind them.

Before you overwrite a factory slot you like, pick another slot: the factory sound cannot be saved first.

Over Bluetooth

On Android and Linux the app also reaches the pedal over Bluetooth. Reading, editing and writing presets over the radio were checked on a pedal; sending an IR or a capture that way was not yet tried on one, so use the cable for the library if you can, and tell us if Bluetooth worked for you.

Questions

Where do I get captures?

TONE3000 hosts free NAM captures of amps and pedals, and the app shows it inside the Library, listing only the A2 captures the pedal can play. See the TONE3000 guide.

My IR is 48 kHz, or stereo. Will it work?

Yes. The app converts it to the pedal's format when you add it, and tells you if a file cannot be read at all.

Can I use a regular NAM file?

Only A2 files. The pedal runs one small, fixed network, and an A2 file already contains it. Any other .nam would have to be retrained to that shape, which the app does not do.

Do I need the latest firmware?

The upload was measured on V1.05. If your pedal runs an older version, update it first with the same app.
